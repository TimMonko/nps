   Napari is a Python library for fast n-dimensional image visualization, annotation, and analysis; the software includes thorough functionality to provide and integrate community developed plugins.

2. [**napari-ndev**](https://github.com/timmonko/napari-ndev)**. Timothy Monko** (2023 — 2026). Lead developer of napari-ndev, a napari plugin intended to serve any person seeking to process microscopy images from start to finish, with no coding necessary. Napari-ndev was designed to address the gap between the napari viewer and batch python scripting. [Documentation](https://timmonko.github.io/napari-ndev/).

# **Research Experience**

## **University of Minnesota,** Department of Pediatrics, Division of Neonatology

	**Thomas W. Bastian, Ph.D.** 2022 – 2026

* Studying the interactions between mitochondrial function and cytoskeletal dynamics in primary neuronal cultures to understand deficits in axon outgrowth and bioenergetics in disease models including iron deficiency. Developing automated high throughput image analysis methods, available publicly as napari-ndev, using machine learning for multivariate spatiotemporal morphological analysis. Collaborations using these techniques have projects spanning embryonic neural development, neuro-immune interactions, and hematologic disorders.

## **University of Minnesota,** Department of Neuroscience 

### **Yasushi Nakagawa, M.D, Ph.D.** 2017 – 2021

* Investigated the roles of thalamocortical axons on regulation of neural progenitor cells  in embryonic neocortical neurogenesis in mouse. Showed that thalamocortical axons provide necessary extrinsic regulation for the proper production and fate of neurons in the neocortex. Further determined the concurrent function and regulation of microglia during neurogenesis by thalamocortical axons.
